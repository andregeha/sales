# Positioning — value propositions by segment

> Grounded in `knowledge/product/`. Lead with the buyer's problem, not our module list.

## The three sentences that do most of the work
1. **"Front-to-back in one system."** Onboarding and KYC, portfolio management, order execution,
   settlement, accounting, fees, reporting and the client portal — one database, one audit trail,
   no integration project between five vendors.
2. **"Compliance is enforced at the point of trade, not discovered afterwards."** Pre-trade
   blocking on every channel including the client portal, plus an end-of-day recheck and a full
   ex-ante/ex-post audit trail.
3. **"Pay only for what you need."** Modular. Start with the essentials, add modules as you grow.

## By segment
### Banks
Lead: **compliance + audit + core-banking integration + branches on the portal**.
Then: multi-environment (own book, client book, funds), the full fee and commission stack including
bank commission and IB retrocession, double-entry accounting and trial balance, SWIFT settlement,
regulatory packs. Proof point: existing bank clients across Lebanon, the Gulf and France.

### Brokers / investment firms
Lead: **OMS + FIX + trading desk**. Order types, validity types, bloc and basket orders with
pro-rata allocation, Bloomberg EMSX or direct broker routing, brokerage-fee setup by broker and
market, back-to-back trades, client vs broker fee legs and the bank-commission margin.

### Family offices & multi-family offices
Lead: **consolidation + the portal + reporting**. Many portfolios, many custodians, many currencies,
one consolidated view with drill-down; a dedicated family-office report; **one EAM login sees the
whole book**; private equity (commitments, capital calls, distributions, IRR) and repos, which most
competitors handle badly.

### Fund managers / fund administration
Lead: **NAV + subscriptions/redemptions + shareholder registry + fees**. Multi-share, multi-currency,
allocation factor, official vs stored NAV, management **and performance/incentive** fees, fund
accounting, periodic statements per subscriber.

### Discretionary / advisory wealth managers
Lead: **model portfolios + rebalancing + suitability + client reporting**. Rebalance to index, grid
or up to five weighted model portfolios; suitability tied to the client's KYC risk score; branded
multi-language client statements delivered by portal, email or batch.

### Existing Gaia clients (upgrade motion)
Lead: **"upgrade to the latest now, move to the New Gaia later."** The latest version is the same
technology they already run, with new features available today — no re-platform risk. The New Gaia
then arrives **on the same database**, screen by screen, with the desktop still working alongside it.

## Differentiators we should say out loud
- **The database is the contract** in the New Gaia — no data migration, no big-bang cutover.
- **A ready-made CMA/BDL regulatory pack** for Lebanon — little to build for a regulated house there.
- **The constraint library is maintained by OFS** — regulatory rules arrive as product, not as a project.
- **Arabic/RTL and multi-language** across the portal and the report engine.
- **Private equity and repos** alongside listed assets in the same portfolio view.
- **A finance-expert support team**, not a generic helpdesk.
- **Cost-effective and modular** — a credible alternative to the tier-one platforms for firms that
  will never use half of what those charge for.
