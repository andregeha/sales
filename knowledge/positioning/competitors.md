## Sightings (real, dated, from lead sourcing)

|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05-07 | **Broadridge** (Investment Management Solutions) | Amwal Capital Partners, DIFC, Dubai | Went live on Broadridge IMS. A DIFC asset manager in our exact target segment, now on a competitor platform. Recorded as a **negative signal** on that record — they are not a near-term prospect. |
| Feb 2025 | **Temenos → Montagu** (Multifonds sold, ~$400m) | Global | Vendor ownership change. Installed bases get unsettled when a platform changes hands — worth watching for displacement opportunities. |

**How to use a sighting:** a firm that just implemented a competitor is *not* a lead — mark it
`nurture` with the system named and the date, and revisit in 2–3 years. What it *is* worth is
intelligence: it tells us who we are losing to, in which segment, in which market.
