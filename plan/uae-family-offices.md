# UAE family offices and MFOs -- named firms not on any regulator register

> Written 2026-09-24, at Andres request. Scope: UAE only, single family offices (SFO) and
> multi-family offices (MFO), sourced from trade press, conference speaker/testimonial pages, and
> firms own websites -- not from DFSA, FSRA or SCA/CMA registers, per the brief (family offices
> are structurally excluded or withdrawn from those registers; see "why registers cannot help" in
> the task that produced this file).
>
> Do not re-run the closed routes -- DFSA Single Family Office category (withdrawn), DIFC private
> register (no public access), Family Wealth Centre (no directory), EFOA (approval-only,
> no-solicitation). See "Dead-end routes" at the bottom for what else produced nothing.
>
> Method. Every firm below has an evidence URL that names it as a family office or MFO -- its own
> site, a named press article, or a conference speaker/sponsor/testimonial page. Nothing is inferred
> from a name alone. AUM figures quoted by third-party data vendors (Preqin, Dakota, Praxis Rock,
> Altss, etc.) are NOT repeated as fact here unless independently corroborated -- those vendors
> were used only to find a name, then checked against a primary source. Where a primary source could
> not be found, the firm is flagged "conference-listing only" and should be treated as lower
> confidence.

## Dead-end / low-yield search routes -- do not repeat

- Generic queries like "multi-family office Dubai website" mostly surface service providers to
  family offices (AES International wealth-management pages, Christie/real-estate guides, "how to
  set up a family office" law-firm content) rather than named operating firms -- useful only as a
  jumping-off point to specific names, never as a source in itself.
- Vendor "top N family offices" listicles (Dakota, Praxis Rock, Altss, FindLPs,
  activefamilyoffices.com) surfaced most of the names above, but their own AUM figures are
  unsourced marketing numbers and were NOT carried into this file -- several conflict with each
  other on the same firm (e.g. Al Ghurair AUM estimates ranging wildly) and one (Dakota) lists MSA
  Capital as a UAE family office when MSA Capital is actually a China-focused venture capital firm
  -- a clear vendor data error. Do not treat any of these vendor pages as a primary source; use
  them only to generate a name to then verify.
- "Al Ghurair Family Office" / "Al Ghurair Investment" -- not pursued past the vendor listicles.
  Al Ghurair is fundamentally an industrial/retail conglomerate (cement, foods, retail); no
  independent primary source was found naming a distinct family-office vehicle in the time
  available. Worth a second pass if this segment is revisited.
- LinkedIn company-page search for "Advantage Family Office" and "Small House Capital" surfaced
  personal profiles (CIOs) but no crawlable public company page distinct from the personal profile
  -- recorded as unknown rather than guessed.
- Citywire Middle East articles are search-indexed but WebFetch-blocked (returns an empty body,
  likely paywall/bot protection) -- every Citywire fact above was recovered via WebSearch snippets
  or a second syndication of the same story (PE Insights, Executive Moves, etc.), never a direct
  fetch.
- equalis.ch (the real Dubai Equalis Capital domain) returned a self-signed-certificate error on
  WebFetch -- reachable in a browser but not via this tool. Facts about it above come from search
  snippets and third-party directory listings, not the firm own site text.
- DIFC/FSRA/DFSA-adjacent commercial directories (Connect.ae, HiDubai, 2GIS, Dun and Bradstreet)
  were useful for confirming an address or legal form but were NOT used as the evidentiary basis
  for "this is a family office" -- that always came from press, a conference page, or the firm own
  site.
