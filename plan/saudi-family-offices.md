# Saudi family offices, MFOs and asset managers -- named firms no regulator register will show

> Written 2026-09-24 at Andre's request. Scope: Saudi Arabia only. Sourced from trade press, the
> families' and firms' own websites, conference/vendor listing pages (used only to find a name, then
> checked against a primary or company-owned source), and GLEIF (used only to verify/enrich a name
> already found elsewhere -- GLEIF carries no industry classification and was never used to source a
> name). CMA, SAMA, Tadawul and open-data-portal coverage is a colleague's parallel workstream --
> not duplicated here. Publicly listed companies (e.g. BinDawood Holding, ACWA Power, Al Baraka
> Banking Group, Zamil Industrial, Sipchem) are Tadawul-covered elsewhere and out of scope as new
> candidates, even where they surface below as a family's better-known subsidiary.
>
> Method and honesty rule applied throughout: a family being wealthy, or a "Holding Company" bearing
> a family name, is not evidence of a family office. Many of Saudi Arabia's largest and oldest named
> merchant groups are operating conglomerates (construction, retail, automotive distribution,
> industrial manufacturing) that a family owns, not investment or asset-management vehicles. Where a
> source explicitly names a separate, distinct investment company or family office entity (e.g. Majd
> Investment vs. Almajdouie Holding; Zahrat Al Amaal vs. Fawaz Alhokair Group), the two are listed and
> distinguished. Where no such distinct vehicle could be found, the group is listed in the "no named
> investment vehicle found" section so nobody re-runs the same search. Vendor "family office" labels
> from Preqin, Altss or SWF Institute alone, with no independent corroboration of a dedicated
> investment function distinct from the operating business, are flagged as weak and not promoted to
> the main table.

## Ownership/regulatory-change flags -- report these up, do not treat as prospects

- Binladin International Holding Group (BIHG), Jeddah -- previously described (Preqin/Altss) as the
  Bin Laden family's single family office, est. 2019, managing construction/real-estate-linked family
  assets. As of a 2026-06-23 report, combined Saudi government ownership exceeds 92%, with the
  Finance Ministry holding 86.38% directly and the founding Bin Laden family reduced to under 8%.
  This is a major, dated ownership change -- BIHG is now a state-controlled vehicle, not a family
  office in any commercially meaningful sense today. Source:
  https://enterpriseam.com/ksa/2026/06/23/exclusive-saudi-govt-now-controls-over-92-of-binladen-group/
  (also see earlier Yahoo Finance/Reuters coverage of the 2018 "shake-up" curbing family influence).
  Do not pursue as a family-office prospect.
- The Saudi Investment Company (SICO), Geneva -- represents the worldwide interests of the Saudi
  Binladin Group; per the same sourcing, the Saudi Ministry of Finance holds 83.38% via Istidama
  Holding, with the Bin Laden family holding only 13.62% via "Binladin Company for Development and
  Commercial Investment." Same pattern as BIHG: majority state-owned, not family-controlled. Not a
  prospect on family-office grounds.

## Name-collision flags -- read before writing to any of these

- "Al Rajhi" / "Alrajhi" names at least four separate things found in this pass, none of which should
  be merged: (1) Al Rajhi Bank, the public bank (regulator-side, out of this brief's scope);
  (2) Sulaiman bin Abdulaziz Alrajhi Holding Company, one specific family branch's SFO, already in
  the CRM; (3) AlRajhi Partners, a 2019-founded VC/PE firm in Riyadh, listed above; and
  (4) "ALRAJHI PARTNERS LIMITED," a separate, inactive, London-registered LEI entity of unclear
  relationship to (3). Do not assume any of these four are the same organisation without a source
  that says so.
- "Al Turki" / "Alturki" names at least two unrelated groups: Khalid Ali Alturki & Sons / Alturki
  Holding (Riyadh-based industrial/construction conglomerate with an internal, unnamed
  corporate-venture-capital unit) and Rawabi Holding Group, founded by Abdulaziz AlTurki (an energy/
  petrochemicals/offshore-marine conglomerate, Al Khobar). Sources give no indication these are the
  same family branch. Treat as two distinct "Al Turki" entities.
- "Juffali" covers at least the operating group Ebrahim A. Juffali & Brothers, the branch vehicle
  Khaled Juffali Company, and Hasma Capital (London/Hong Kong, "the family office for the Juffali
  clan" per Caproasia) -- domiciled outside Saudi Arabia and outside this brief's scope, but flagged
