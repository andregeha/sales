# UAE asset/wealth managers found by web search -- outside DIFC and ADGM registers

> Written 2026-09-24, at Andre's request. Scope: UAE asset managers, wealth managers and investment
> firms that do NOT appear on the DFSA (DIFC) or FSRA (ADGM) registers we already hold -- i.e. firms
> operating from DMCC, DWTCA, RAK ICC, Meydan, IFZA, Dubai Silicon Oasis, the mainland (UAE CMA,
> formerly SCA), or under the UAE Insurance Authority, or with no visible licence at all.
>
> Method. Many varied web searches (English and Arabic), followed by opening every firm's own
> website (or, where that failed, the best available primary/secondary source) to check it is a real
> operating business and read its own description of itself. Directory/listicle pages (Vault,
> Orpheus, Rudra, Reabus, Fiducia Adamantina, Citywealth's Top 30) were used only to surface names,
> never as evidence in themselves -- most of what they list turned out to be either global private
> banks (out of scope -- not our segment/size) or firms already on a register we hold.
>
> Cross-checked against the CRM before including anything. The following names surfaced by search
> were confirmed to be ALREADY in the CRM (via the DFSA, FSRA or UAE-CMA/onshore connectors) and are
> therefore NOT repeated below: HK Asset Management, Introspect Capital, Index and Cie, Prometheus,
> GSB Capital, Vault Wealth, Neovision (Wealth Management and Investment Fund Management), Century
> Financial Consultancy / Century Private Wealth, Al Mal Capital, Allied Investment Partners, SHUAA
> Capital, Noor Capital, Al Ramz Capital / Corporation. This is a good sign for the existing
> registers' coverage -- it means the search-then-verify method is finding the same real firms the
> connectors already found, not a different population, for anything that genuinely holds a
> DIFC/ADGM/onshore-CMA licence.
>
> Regulatory-change note carried over from plan/uae-sources-onshore.md: the UAE Securities and
> Commodities Authority (SCA) was renamed the Capital Market Authority (CMA) of the UAE effective 1
> January 2026. Any mainland ("SCA-licensed") firm below is licensed by this successor body; do not
> confuse with the unrelated Saudi CMA.

## What did NOT open a new channel, and why

- DMCC does not license "asset management" as a regulated activity at all. Its own material is
  explicit that asset holding/structuring goes through SPV and Holding Company licences, and
  regulated financial services still route through DIFC or ADGM. Every DMCC-based name found in this
  session that used the words "asset management" or "fund management" in its own marketing
  (Richcomm, Dubai Commodity/Shariah Asset Management, several gold/commodity traders) turned out to
  be either unreachable, a physical-commodity trading business, or a proprietary trader -- not a
  third-party asset/portfolio manager in our sense. This matches, rather than contradicts, the
  finding in plan/uae-sources-freezone.md that DMCC's own directory is both technically hard to read
  and contractually off-limits for our use anyway.
- RAK ICC is a corporate/holding-company registry, not a place operating wealth managers are
  physically based. Everything found there was structuring language (foundations, holding companies,
  trusts) aimed at using RAK ICC as a vehicle jurisdiction, not a directory of firms that manage money
  from Ras Al Khaimah. Not pursued further as a hunting ground for this brief.
- Meydan Free Zone and IFZA/Dubai Silicon Oasis readily issue "Investment Advisory Services"
  (activity 6619.02) and similar licences, and business-setup content confirms this explicitly, but
  no named, verifiable operating advisory firm using either free zone was found and confirmed this
  session beyond MudraUAE (unverified, see Rejected table) -- this remains a real, open gap, not a
  closed one; a further pass focused on Meydan/IFZA company-formation case studies and press might
  surface named firms that this session's queries did not.
- Prop-trading and quant firms found in DMCC (iBloxx, Fin Rise DMCC, Quant Lab SFO FZCO) trade
  proprietary/family capital, not third-party client portfolios -- out of scope for a portfolio and
  fund management sales pitch even though they are real operating businesses; not carried into
  either table above.
- Sovereign/government investment arms beyond Sharjah Asset Management (e.g. RAK Investment
  Authority/RAKIA) were seen but not pursued -- they are government economic-development bodies, not
  the private-sector asset/wealth-manager segment this brief targets, and RAKIA's own site was not
  opened this session.
