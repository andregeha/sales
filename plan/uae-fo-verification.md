# DFSA "Single Family Office" list (Dubai, 2023 snapshot) - 41-firm verification


**Source of the list.** DFSA public register (`https://www.dfsa.ae/public-register/firms`), category
"Single Family Office," all 41 entries marked **Withdrawn**. The *category* was withdrawn in 2023 when
DIFC moved single-family offices to its Family Wealth Centre - the centre publishes no directory - so
this list is a frozen 2023-era snapshot, the only enumerable list of named Gulf family offices found
anywhere. Each name below was checked independently against public sources on 2026-09-24.

**Method.** GLEIF LEI lookups (`api.gleif.org`, fulltext filter), web search, and third-party mirrors of
the DIFC public register (b2bhint, connect.ae, 996co, decypha, Altss, Preqin, SWFI) - the DIFC
register itself (`difc.ae` / `difc.com`) sits behind a Vercel bot-checkpoint and could not be read
directly; no attempt was made to defeat it. Register mirrors are dated snapshots, not live status -
flagged explicitly wherever that matters.

**Result at a glance: of 41 names, 19 are confirmed active, going-concern single-family or
multi-family offices in Dubai; 5 more clearly still exist but are not classic investment-managing
family offices (holding companies for an operating business, a PE/advisory shop, or a Singapore-seated
family whose Dubai vehicle's current role is unclear); 3 are confirmed closed or dissolved; 14 remain
unconfirmed - no independent evidence of current existence was found beyond the frozen DFSA/DIFC
listing itself.**
