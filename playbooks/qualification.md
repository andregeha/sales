# Qualification

> A deal is qualified when we can answer these without guessing. Anything we cannot answer is a
> **risk**, and it goes in `memory/open-questions.md` or the account's engagement file.

| Dimension | The question | Where the answer lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | What is broken, what does it cost them, and who feels it? | `accounts/<slug>/profile.md` |
| **Impact** | What changes for the business if we fix it? Quantified in their terms. | engagement.md |
| **Decision process** | Who decides, who influences, who can veto, what are the steps? | profile.md → decision map |
| **Economic buyer** | Have we met them, or are we talking to someone who cannot say yes? | profile.md |
| **Champion** | Is there someone who wants this to happen and will spend capital on it? | profile.md |
| **Criteria** | What will they evaluate us on, and did we help shape that list? | engagement.md |
| **Timeline** | What forces a decision by a date — and is it real or aspirational? | engagement.md |
| **Budget** | Does money exist, in which budget, and for which financial year? | Andre |
| **Competition** | Who else, including the incumbent and "do nothing"? | engagement.md |
| **Fit** | Can Gaia genuinely do what they need, without heroics? | `knowledge/product/` |
| **Risk** | What is most likely to kill this, and what are we doing about it? | engagement.md |

## Honest disqualification
Walking away early is cheaper than losing late. Disqualify when:
- There is no economic buyer we can reach, and no path to one.
- The timeline is aspirational and nothing forces it.
- The requirement needs capability we do not have, and building it is not commercially sensible.
- They want a price before they will discuss scope, and will not move.

Say so plainly in the account file, park it in the pipeline with the reason, and move on.

## The question people forget
**"What happens if you do nothing?"** If the answer is "not much", there is no deal yet —
there is a conversation about why the status quo is more expensive than they think.
