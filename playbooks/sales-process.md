# Sales process

> The default motion. Deviate deliberately, not accidentally — and write down why.

## 1. Identify
Reason to believe: a regulatory driver, a system end-of-life, a new licence, a launch, growth past
what spreadsheets survive, an incumbent failing them, or an existing client with an unserved need.
→ `accounts/<slug>/profile.md`.

## 2. Research before contact
Entity, segment, regulator, size, systems, people, and **what changed recently**. Never walk in cold
when twenty minutes of reading would have made us credible. → the `ofs-researcher` agent.

## 3. Discovery
Listen more than pitch. Understand the business before mapping modules.
→ `playbooks/discovery.md` and the `/discovery-call` skill.

## 4. Qualify
Need · decision process · people · timeline · budget · competition · what happens if they do nothing.
→ `playbooks/qualification.md` and the `/deal-qualification` skill.

## 5. Demonstrate
Show the shortest path from their stated pain to a working screen. The **live web portal** is
usually the strongest single asset. → `playbooks/demo.md`.

## 6. Propose
Scope, modules, phasing, delivery approach, timeline, commercials.
⚠ **Commercials always go through Andre.**

## 7. Close and hand over
Signed scope → delivery. Write down what we promised, so delivery inherits the truth and not a
cheerful summary.

## 8. Expand
Existing clients are the best pipeline we have: version upgrades, extra modules, extra environments,
the portal, and the New Gaia transition. Finance House and GIMD are both this motion.

## Standing principles
- **Two information sets, never mixed** — what we sell today vs what they currently run.
- **Never quote a price.** Ever. Scope first, Andre second.
- **Never commit to a date or a feature** that is not in `knowledge/`.
- **Write it down the same day.** A meeting not recorded is a meeting half wasted.
- **Say "let me confirm"** instead of guessing. Then actually confirm, and log it.
