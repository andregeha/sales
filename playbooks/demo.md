# Demo playbook

> A demo is an argument, not a tour. Show the shortest path from their stated pain to a working
> screen, and stop.

## Before
1. Re-read `accounts/<slug>/profile.md` — what did they actually say they cared about?
2. Pick **three things** to show. Not eight.
3. Decide the one sentence you want them repeating to their colleague afterwards.
4. Check the environment works **the day before**. A failed demo costs more than a postponed one.
5. Get Andre's approval on anything commercial, and on any client name used as a reference.

## The strongest openers by audience
| Audience | Open with |
|---|---|
| Business / owner / principal | The **web portal** — it is modern, live today, and instantly legible |
| Compliance officer | A **pre-trade breach**: place an order that trips a limit, show it blocked, show the audit line |
| Operations / back office | **Reconciliation** and the **validation ladder**, then the fee engine |
| Trader / dealing desk | The **trading desk blotter**, order types and validity, then FIX routing |
| IT / architecture | The **New Gaia** story — same database, additive changes, desktop and web coexisting |
| Fund administrator | **NAV** per share class, subscriptions/redemptions, management & performance fees |

## The portal demo — our best asset
It defuses "your product is a desktop application" in about ninety seconds.
Sequence: log in as a **manager** → the whole book consolidated → drill into one client →
positions with unrealized P&L → **place an order** and let the constraint engine check it →
the **KYC document centre** with expiry and mandatory flags → **generate a client statement**.
That is the entire lifecycle in one browser tab.

## Rules
- **Never demo something you have not run yourself that week.**
- If something breaks, say what it is and move on. Do not narrate around it.
- If asked for a feature you are unsure about: *"Let me confirm that precisely rather than give you
  an approximate answer."* Then log it in `memory/open-questions.md` and actually come back.
- **No internal references on screen** — no screen numbers, table names, other clients' data.
- Send a written follow-up the same day: what we showed, what we promised, what happens next.
