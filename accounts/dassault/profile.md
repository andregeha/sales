## Our angle
An existing client **mid-upgrade**, addressed as a family office — no bank, agency, branch or
retail-client framing. The story is a controlled upgrade (production copy → upgrade → screen-by-screen
validation → non-regression → cutover, zero data risk), full support (training, assisted acceptance
testing, milestones), and then the direction of travel toward the New Gaia.
