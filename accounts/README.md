# Accounts — existing clients (context and proof, NOT pipeline)

⚠ **This sales workspace is scoped to new business.** The folders here are **existing OFS clients**,
kept because they are the best proof we have of what Gaia actually does in production — and because
mixing them into the new-business pipeline would make the pipeline a lie.

**New-business prospects live in the CRM** (`crm/companies/`), not here.

## Why these are still worth keeping
- **Proof points** — a real bank, a real family office, a real retail investing platform, all
  running Gaia today. Enormously useful when a prospect asks "who else uses this?"
  ⚠ **Naming any of them to a prospect needs Andre's explicit approval, every time.**
- **Pattern library** — what these firms actually needed, and what won them, informs how we pitch
  the next one in the same segment.
- **Adjacency** — their people move, and their networks are the warmest route into a market this
  small. Any such route goes through Andre.

## Rules
- Keep an existing client's **installed version** strictly separate from **what we sell today**.
  This is golden rule #1 and it is easiest to break here.

  quietly running a deal out of this folder.
