## The "six modules" framing — BLOM-specific
⚠ **This arrangement is how BLOM conceives of the product. Other clients organize differently.**
Do not present this as the canonical module list.
1. **Client Portfolio** — the core/essentials module: the real-time single-mandate cockpit for one
   client's portfolio or strategy in its base currency. Everything else builds on this.
2. **Mutual Fund** — fund administration: subscriptions/redemptions, shareholder registry, EOD NAV
   per share class, fund accounting, management & performance fees.
3. **Bank Portfolio** — the bank's own multi-asset book, leading on stocks and fixed income with
   amortization, revaluation and P&L; full fixed-income analytics underneath.
4. **Private Equity** — commitments, capital calls, distributions, NAV estimation, DPI/TVPI/IRR.
5. **Family Office** — consolidation across many portfolios, custodians and currencies, with
   drill-down and a dedicated family-office report.
6. **Web Portal** — for **branches, management and clients**. Placed last as the delivery layer.

## Conventions specific to this account
- The word **"agencies" is deliberately avoided** — say **branches**.
- The portal slide reads "Secure web access for branches, management and clients", and the feature
  list includes "**Send and track orders**".
- Thank-you slide uses the **four offices** (Paris · Riyadh · Beirut · Dubai), not the standard two.
