# Competitive battlecard

⚠ `knowledge/positioning/competitors.md` is currently a **placeholder** — we have no verified
competitive intelligence. Start by reading it, and by asking Andre the questions listed there.

## Build it
1. **Who they are** — segment, geography, typical deal size, where they are strong. Sourced.
2. **Where we genuinely win** — with evidence, not assertion. Our defensible ground:
   - **Scope for the money** — modular, pay only for what you need.
   - **Local regulatory coverage** — the constraint library is maintained by OFS as product,
     including a ready-made CMA/BDL pack.
   - **The no-migration re-platform story** — the New Gaia runs on the unchanged database.
   - **Private equity and repos** alongside listed assets in one portfolio view.
   - **A finance-expert support team**, not a ticket queue.
   - **Arabic/RTL and multi-language** across the portal and report engine.
3. **Where we lose** — honestly. If we cannot say this, the card is marketing, not intelligence.
4. **Traps to avoid** — where a feature comparison flatters them.
5. **Questions that favour us** — things worth putting in front of a buyer building an
   evaluation list, phrased as genuine questions, never as attacks.

## Rules
- **Never disparage a named competitor to a client.** Describe our capability and let them compare.
- **Never state an unverified fact about a competitor.** Pricing, roadmap and customer claims
  especially — being wrong about a rival destroys credibility faster than anything else.
- Every entry is sourced and dated. Competitive facts rot quickly.
- If asked about a competitor and we do not know: *"I'd rather show you what we do than
  characterize someone else's product."*
