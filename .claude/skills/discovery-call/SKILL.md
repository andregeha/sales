# Discovery call

## Preparing
1. Read `accounts/<slug>/` and `playbooks/discovery.md`.
2. Select the questions that matter **for this account** — never read the full list aloud.
   Group A (the business) always comes first; it shapes everything else.
3. Anticipate: which modules will likely lead, which objections are likely
   (`knowledge/positioning/objection-handling.md`), which proof asset to have ready.
4. Decide the **one thing** we must not leave without knowing.
5. Prepare what *we* must be ready to answer — and confirm anything shaky with Andre **first**.

## Writing it up — do this the same day
1. **Who was there**, their roles, and who was notably absent.
2. **What they told us** — in their words. Separate what they *said* from what we *inferred*.
3. **What we showed or claimed** — so delivery inherits the truth, not a cheerful summary.
4. **What we committed to**, with owners and dates.
5. **What they owe us**, with dates.
6. **What we learned that changes our view** — new facts, killed assumptions.
7. **Next action** — one person, one verb, one date.

## Persist
`accounts/<slug>/engagement.md` (timeline + open items) · new durable facts → `memory/facts.md` ·
new unknowns → `memory/open-questions.md` · stage or next action changed → `pipeline/pipeline.md`.

## Rules
- Record what was **actually said**, not what we wish had been said.
- Any commitment made on a price, date or scope is flagged for **Andre immediately**.
- If something was promised that we are not sure we can do, that is an open question **today**,
  not a problem for later.
